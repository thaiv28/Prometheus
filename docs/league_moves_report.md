# League moves by standing

league_moves.py: convert a player's Elo on a move between leagues by their standing in
the old league instead of by league offsets, then retest linking leagues at
non-international cross-league games (EMEA Masters, cups, promotion).

Published Elo keeps a moving player's rating plus offset: a player at 1,450 in an ERL is
1,450 in the LEC. Linking ERLs at EMEA Masters moved their offsets and those offsets came
into the majors with promoted players, hurting major-league forecasts (work log
2026-10-06). The variant here gives a mover the new league's active average plus
`move_weight` times their distance from the old league's active average, so offsets no
longer travel with players and only decide team-versus-team calls across leagues.

Fixed before the run: move_weight 1 (no tuning), link shares 0.25 and 0.5 (the earlier
test's values), scope all moves or only moves touching a non-major league. Scored like
`evaluate_metrics.py`: domestic and international (Elo and FORGE), cross-league in every
league, and within other leagues (per-league curves), each paired against published Elo.

    git checkout 26d774b && uv run python scripts/league_moves.py --out docs/league_moves_report.md

The harness and the experimental switches live only on the unmerged `league-move` branch (PR #31, closed); run it from that commit. Elo on `main` is unchanged.

On macOS set VECLIB_MAXIMUM_THREADS=1.

## Findings

- **Converting by standing is worse everywhere** (every scope, with or without links): domestic Elo +0.007, within other leagues +0.011, cross-league +0.026 without links, all significant. Players who move between leagues and keep their rating are the main thing that ties minor leagues (and tiers) to each other; converting by standing removes that tie.
- **Links used only for team ratings work.** Non-international cross-league games (EMEA Masters, cups, promotion) move a second, team-only offset; a moving player still converts by the international offsets, as today. At share 0.25: cross-league −0.0123 (−0.0151 to −0.0095), from 2022 −0.0187 (−0.0232 to −0.0141); domestic Elo −0.0002 (−0.0005 to +0.0000), FORGE domestic −0.0002 (−0.0003 to −0.0000); international +0.0012 (−0.0004 to +0.0028), from 2022 +0.0009 (−0.0012 to +0.0030); within other leagues +0.0002 (−0.0001 to +0.0004). Share 0.5 gains a bit more across leagues but is slightly, significantly worse within other leagues (+0.0003).
- Plain links at 0.25 (the earlier test) reproduce: cross-league −0.0118, but domestic Elo from 2022 +0.0008 (+0.0002 to +0.0014), the problem the team-only offset removes.

Each cell: log loss (variant − published, paired 95% interval); ** = interval excludes 0.
