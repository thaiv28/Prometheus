## 2026-10-02 — Tighter AURA rule; held-out combined test; team-centred late change adopted

Passes. V = 15 + vision/frontline gaps: split-half +0.091 but teammates +0.080 and equal to its team-only control (−0.000, −0.025 to +0.024); new team +0.011 (n.s.; vs control +0.065, −0.007 to +0.132). Fails. SV (both): vs control split-half +0.008 (n.s.), teammates +0.080. Fails. ECE out of year: 15 min 0.0086, 25 min 0.0069.
- **Adopted S** in `prometheus/aura.py`: `get_aura` = 15-minute term + `LATE_WEIGHT` (0.25) × `team_centred` change to `LATE_MINUTES` (25, else 20); new `snapshot_scores`, `earlier_only`, `MIN_TRAIN_GAMES`. `evaluate_aura.py`: AURA is now the chosen score (snapshot terms alone are comparisons), new held-out section (`held_out`), `split_halves`/`season_pairs` take a score list, `roster_table` a score.
