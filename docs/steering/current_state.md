# Current state

Last reviewed: 2026-10-03 (Predictions page). This is a code baseline. Read `db/prometheus.db` or the live site for current rankings.

## Working

- **Research (not published):** in-game snapshots, champions, snapshot and visible-input Elo, the gol.gg objective corpus and benchmarks, and the broadcast OCR pilot. Status and findings are in [`docs/research/README.md`](../research/README.md#status).

- **Data:** Oracle's Elixir CSVs for 2014 to the present, rebuilt from scratch by `scripts/setup_db.sh` and refreshed daily in CI (downloaded at most once per day on `main` and cached; every other run, and every PR, builds from the cache). International events (Worlds, MSI, EWC, First Stand, and since 2026-10-06 the Demacia Cup Global Invitational `DCGI` and World Star Challengers Invitational `WSCI`, ...) are ingested as their own leagues (`INTERNATIONAL_LEAGUES`); they are the only games that connect regions, and never a team's home league.
- **Two families.** Season stats describe a team-season with hindsight and are judged by `scripts/evaluate_season_stats.py` (stability and fit to that season's results; `docs/season_stats_report.md`). Forecasts predict the next game from earlier games only and are judged by `scripts/evaluate_metrics.py` (`docs/metric_backtest.md`). No forecast uses a season stat as input.
- **Season stats** (LCK/LPL/LEC/LCS team-seasons):
  - GLORY: per-year regression weights on gold and objective rates, with every game's stats adjusted for the opponent's full-season Record; about 0–100, with Era Z and League Z. Full-season reliability 0.91; about 8 games to reach 0.5.
  - Record (sunset 2026-10-01; page kept, still GLORY's opponent-strength input): a schedule-adjusted results rating (Bradley-Terry on game-length-weighted results, every league including international events), shown as the chance to beat the average major-league team that year. Reliability 0.89.
  - Luck (sunset 2026-10-01; page kept): wins above what a team's play earned (GLORY's per-game model, calibrated per season). Reliability 0.34, so it is mostly luck; it correlates 0.23 with win %.
  - Sunset (`sunset.html`, linked from the header's Teams menu): Record, Luck, GLORB, GLORY unadjusted (`glory_unadjusted.html`), Form (folded into FORGE), and GLORY+ (folded into GLORY; `glory_plus.html` redirects).

Legacy international records remain original; earlier unposted minor picks remain a separate archive. Pending/unresolved picks are ungraded. - **Forecasts:**
  - **International calibration (release 2026-10-15, enabled by default):** two separate purposes: Elo's per-game regional offset updates rating strength and follows players; an offline fitted regional calibration corrects remaining international forecast error. Only recognized cross-home international fixtures receive it.   - Game-length Elo for every team in every region, built from player ratings: a team's rating is the average of its five starters plus its league offset; new players start at the average of their league's active players (last game within 365 days) and count 250 points lower in their first game, the discount fading with log games to nothing at 200, with their rating moving twice as fast for their first 25 (2026-10-08, log curve 2026-10-12). A player back from time away moves up to 3× the team's change in their first game back, and a player moving down from a major gains 25 points (2026-10-12). League offsets move with cross-league international games. A team's home league is its most-played league this year, and forecasts rate it by its main roster (its last home-league or international lineup). Domestic log loss 0.6315 against 0.6348 for team Elo without players (−0.0032, −0.0056 to −0.0010); cross-league 0.5940 (0.6015 before the 2026-10-12 changes).

Every team in every league. Alone it does not beat Elo (domestic log loss +0.0014, −0.0010 to +0.0038; international +0.0450).

The page (2026-10-07) lists every league's teams, each league on its own scale (its blend weights; the majors share one), one league at a time, majors by default; the head to head uses the pair's league slope. Weights in `prometheus/forge_weights.json` and `form_weights.json`, written by `--write-weights`; CI refits and warns when a blend weight moves more than 10%.

- **AURA (player season stat, on the site since 2026-10-02):** `aura.html` lists 2,503 major-league player-seasons with 20+ games (a season needs about 22 games to reach 0.5 reliability), in win-chance points per game (25 × log-odds) with Role Z, role rank and a role filter; player pages show AURA by season. `prometheus/aura.py` scores each starter's share of a game: a logistic win-probability model on the five lanes' gaps to their lane opponent at 15 minutes (gold, XP, CS, kills, deaths, assists; one weight per role and stat, fit per year), and a player's AURA is their lane's 15-minute log-odds term plus a quarter of their team-centred change to 25 minutes (own change minus the teammates' average; 20 minutes if the game ended first). Snapshot models calibrated out of year (ECE 0.009 at 15, 0.007 at 25). In major-league player-seasons: split-half 0.59, r with teammates 0.24, next season on a new team 0.48 (lane gold gap 0.42, win % 0.27). Held out (weights from earlier years), the team-centred change beat the 15-minute term alone: new team +0.028 (+0.012 to +0.043), split-half +0.026. Summed over a team's lineups, players' AURA from one half predicts the other half's win % at 0.65, the same as the team's own AURA (0.66) but below GLORY (0.71, −0.058, −0.087 to −0.030) and win % (0.71): 15-minute lanes miss what decides the rest of the game. On one-starter substitutions, AURA so far adds nothing beyond Player Elo (r −0.006, −0.023 to +0.012). `scripts/evaluate_aura.py` → `docs/aura_report.md`; AURA's change rule is in `AGENTS.md`.

Held out (2024 on), these beat one 4-game half-life for every stat on kills (−0.0008 log score per map, −0.0035 on Bo5 maps 1–3 totals), assists (−0.0009) and deaths (−0.0033), all intervals excluding 0, and keep CS's best; a 64-game half-life is worse everywhere. ## Known limits

- **Data refresh depends on Google Drive.** Drive refuses Oracle's Elixir's shared CSVs when they've been downloaded too often ("Too many users"); every daily run from 2 to 7 Oct was refused, and the site's data advanced only when a download got through. Since 2026-10-07 CI downloads at most once a day and only the current year's file (all files on Mondays), one file at a time, so an older year's refusal no longer blocks the current one; but Drive's limit counts every downloader, so a refused day still means yesterday's data. The home and Predictions pages say how far the data goes ("Games through"), the build warns when settled matches are missing from it, and Kalshi alerts skip calls made without a team's latest result.
- **The PR benchmark check guards only the "not significantly worse" half** of the change rules; whether a change earns a significant gain is still read from the reports. It compares nothing until it is on `main` (the base needs `--dump`).
- Season stats cover only the four major leagues. Whether to extend them is an open product decision (`PRODUCT.md`). Form, Elo and FORGE cover every league.
- Elo links regions only through international games. League offsets carry those results to every team in a region, but a league that rarely plays abroad still has a weakly measured offset. K=20, a between-season pull to the mean and an international K multiplier were tested and not adopted (see the work log).
- Form doesn't compare across regions (stats in a weaker league look better), so it is league-relative and FORGE falls back to Elo between leagues. FORGE therefore adds nothing over Elo for international games; the old GLORY+-based blend was 0.0007 better there (not significant).
- FORGE ratings compare only within one league's scale (the majors share one), so the page shows one at a time; cross-league odds come from Elo. A name search lists teams from several leagues together, ranked within their own.
- Form's hyperparameters (half-life 20, carry 0.5, prior 5) were picked on the same backtest that reports them (a coarse 27-point grid; shrinkage barely mattered). Form's opponent adjustment and weights use only earlier or other seasons, but the blend curve's out-of-year fit is the only guard on the hyperparameters.
- Luck's earned win % comes from a linear per-game model; the season calibration removes its pull toward 50%, but Luck still repeats a little (0.34), so part of it is skill the stats miss.
- Record and Luck count games differently: Record counts every game that season (any league), Luck only major-league games.
- The 5-game minimum stays for every season stat, although the report says GLORY needs about 8 games and win % about 16 to reach 0.5 reliability.
- CI only warns when forecast weights drift; someone still has to run `--write-weights` and commit the files.
- A team page's current FORGE (fact line) uses today's Elo and Form, while its season row for this year uses the state after its last game; they can differ slightly.
- The CLI's `--sort-by` applies to GLORY and GLORB only.
- Player Elo moves all five starters together, so it records how a player's teams did and carries it through transfers; it does not split credit within a team. The player register and pages cover 4,854 players (ever in a major league or at an international event, or active in the last two years); past seasons in the register list only major-league player-seasons with 10+ games, to keep the page about 200KB gzipped.
- The site is about 290MB (4,854 player pages, 2,087 team pages, and 117MB of game-log JSON since 2026-10-06). Since 2026-10-07 a deploy uploads only the files whose content changed (`scripts/deploy_site.py`), but a page still changes whenever anything on it does; an immediate rebuild with the same data sent 11 of 11,182 files (2026-10-07); a day with new games hasn't been measured yet.
- **Game logs.** Series are inferred (a team's consecutive games against one opponent within 12 hours), and so is the best-of behind the series chance (a 2–0 Bo2 is called as a Bo3; a 1–1 gets no series chance). Pre-game calls use today's published weights for every past game. Kalshi prices on past series come from `data/market_prices.json`, refreshed only by rerunning `evaluate_markets.py` and `export_market_prices.py` and uploading the file; without it in CI, logs show only prices saved in the prediction log.
- AURA credits a lane, not a player's whole game: a jungler's or support's score is their gap to the opposing jungler or support, kills and assists are shared across teammates, and with gold in the model the deaths weights come out positive (dying while staying even in gold is not penalized). Oracle's Elixir has no snapshot data for LPL games in 2016–2017 and 2022–2025 and about half of 2021; the refreshed local 2026 raw file has complete 15-minute snapshots for 542/674 games (about 20% missing). LPL players without snapshots are absent from AURA, so its comparison population depends on coverage. The existing DB/site may reflect an older raw-data refresh. Full-game stats (vision, damage, gold and kill shares) were tested on 2026-10-02 and mostly restate the team; within-team shares measure the role a team gives a player and don't travel (see the work log). The substitution test is confounded by how teams choose substitutes: the AURA difference doesn't predict even the raw result in swap games (r −0.011), though AURA so far predicts results in ordinary games (team gap r 0.29).
- A season is a calendar year (since 2026-10-07; `matches.year` was Oracle's Elixir's season label, which files autumn promotion and qualifier games under the next year). Those games now count toward the team's current season.
- 13 games still have only one team row (both teams without an id, or other gaps); they get no Elo. Until 2026-10-07 it was about 1,850, from teams Oracle's Elixir gives no id (now `name:<teamname>`).
- The `before` date filter in `get_matches_frame` is exercised only by the backtest, not by its own test.
- **Predictions.** Leaguepedia rate-limits anonymous clients after a few quick calls and keeps refusing for several minutes; CI shares addresses with other users; the bot-password secrets are set (2026-10-06) for a higher limit, but a day's fetch can still fail (the build then keeps yesterday's calls and results). Matches with a team Oracle's Elixir doesn't cover can't be rated (about half of all scheduled matches, nearly all in small leagues). Name matching depends on Leaguepedia and Oracle's Elixir names agreeing after folding; the three exceptions found so far are in `team_aliases.json`, and new ones need adding by hand (the build prints unmatched names). The calls use the data through the day before the build, and the build runs once a day, so a match may be called on ratings a day or two old and teams' rosters for that match are assumed to be their last ones. Reconstructed calls use earlier games only, but the forecast weights were fit on data that includes them. Series odds use a beta-binomial with one within-series correlation for series between two major-league teams and one for the rest (`schedule.SERIES_RHO`); calls saved before 2026-10-08 used independent games. The whole log is rendered into `predictions.html` (about 650KB for 440 past matches), so it will need paging once it holds many months. League labels for events outside `EVENT_LEAGUES` come from the teams' home leagues; a new cross-region event missing from `INTERNATIONAL_LEAGUES` becomes the home league of the teams that play it (as DCGI and WSCI did until 2026-10-06), which turns their FORGE calls into Elo calls and drops them from FORGE and alerts.
- **Kalshi alerts** run about hourly (GitHub may start scheduled runs late, and an hour is skipped while the site is publishing); prices can move before you see the email. The calls they compare against still come from the daily build. The issues are public (the repo is). The 5-point bar and the 6-hour minimum were set from one backtest that tried several strategies. The 36-hour cap was dropped on 2026-10-12: the market report's "Alerts by lead time" found major-league FORGE value bets as good at 48 hours as at 12 (ROI about +12%, interval spanning 0) with higher CLV (+3.3 points against +1.5), and at 72 hours better still on fewer matches. None of it is significant on its own; the alerts' forward CLV is the check. The 6-hour minimum: inside it the market's lead over us grows and other-league value bets lose about 9%, but for major-league FORGE the backtest can't tell 6 hours from 3.
- **Kalshi prices on the page** come from `kalshi.json`, refreshed about hourly (Kalshi's API refuses in-browser requests); without JS a visitor sees the daily build's prices; matches logged before 2026-10-06 have none. Whether Kalshi's Developer Agreement allows republishing prices hasn't been checked (its page refused the fetch).
- **Market benchmark.** Our calls in it are reconstructed (ratings from the day before) with forecast weights fit on data that includes those matches, which flatters us a little. Kalshi's start times for some markets give only the day (taken as midnight Eastern, so the "close" price is earlier than it could be). 52 Kalshi team names are still unmatched after the aliases, nearly all teams Oracle's Elixir lacks (national teams, small ERLs). Six series where Kalshi's result disagreed with our games were dropped (cause not checked). Candlestick fetching is slow on a cold cache (Kalshi refuses bursts).
- e2e tests need a built DB, so they only run locally and in the publish workflow after `setup_db.sh`.

- The live worker has run in production only between matches: no match has yet been collected, woken or published there. The worker wakes from the prediction log, so a match the log lacks, or one starting more than about an hour after the last hourly run, is picked up late. The in-game model is a research preview (not adopted; see product.md), and its game time is estimated: pauses before the service joined a game go undetected. Pregame Elo and FORGE use the latest DB, so a series' earlier maps that day aren't counted. Quotes start when a game is tracked, so a map whose draft began before the service saw it has no pre-map Kalshi price. Some official streams block embedding, and Twitch embeds need the page's host name as `parent` (works on the site and on `localhost`, not on `127.0.0.1`).
