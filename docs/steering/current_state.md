## Working

- **Other games:** local version-1 JSON feeds render game predictions, live scores, monthly results and downloads with tier filters. DB-free `games.yml` restores S3 feeds and uploads by hash; publish restores the same feeds for navigation. GitHub execution and external dispatch remain unverified.

- **Fresh calls:** hourly prices resumes published Elo with Leaguepedia and worker maps; the worker uploads finished maps and dispatches prices (five-minute debounce). Hourly runs also render Predictions/results and feed refreshed live ratings every five minutes. Registers, head-to-head and home ratings use an hourly build-matched overlay; Form and OE dates stay intact. Fake-based checks cover the handoff; live token/SSM grant and deploy pending.

- **Live-map execution:** live with shared capital, full Kelly, 5% map / 10% map-series caps (validator maxima; pregame $250 order, $1k exposure, $2k day) and same-team filled-contract offsets across strategies; uses 30-second last-trade stability, an earlier-only model/market blend and 5-cent net edge during minutes 5–30. A private outbox feeds an immediate SQS executor with map/Bo1 validation and shared bankroll, with separate map event fences; Bo1s use KXLOLGAME after a KXLOLMAP-…-1 absence check; unarmed maps journal reasons; historical kernel parity passes, but actual asks, fills and latency remain unverified. [Rollout guide](../live_map_execution.md); draft v2 failed the trading benchmark and is not wired in ([evidence](../research/live_map_draft_trading.md)).
- **Live in-game model:** deployed objective counts plus three-minute gold momentum, retaining v1 pre-map calls and missing-counter fallback; worker/model identity, runtime smoke and active publishing verified. Estimated clock and prospective calibration remain limits (ECE 0.0182); no draft or participant input shipped. [Release validation](../research/live_model_release.md).
- **Live pages and lifecycle:** the EC2 worker discovers Riot matches. Feed lag backs off on HTTP 400; other window errors retry for 30 polls, and failed games retry after five minutes. Predictions lists matches in progress (`live.html` redirects there) and Watch carries streams, model/Kalshi odds and map charts, and persists started/completed series for seven days through shutdown; recorded results link to Watch before daily archiving. Waiting fixtures appear from 75 minutes before start through four hours late; daily series pages and monthly indexes include recorded maps and marked Kalshi minute-price backfills, while conflicting kill counters leave kills unknown without discarding gold/objectives. Series-score shifts move pre-map and in-game calls and Live results' map odds; they improve pre-map calls on priced maps ([anchor report](../research/live_anchor.md)) but were flat in-game on feed tests ([series checkpoint](../research/series_checkpoint.md), [v2 report](../research/time_model_v2.md)); operation and archive details: [deployment](deployment.md).
- **Research:** unpublished snapshots, champions, snapshot/visible-input Elo, gol.gg objectives and broadcast OCR are indexed in the [research status](../research/README.md#status).
- **Data:** Oracle's Elixir CSVs from 2014 onward are rebuilt by `scripts/setup_db.sh`, refreshed daily on `main` and cached for other runs/PRs. International events, including `DCGI` and `WSCI`, must remain in `INTERNATIONAL_LEAGUES`: they connect regional pools and must never become a team's home league. [Pipeline](tech.md).
- **Metric families:** season stats use hindsight; forecasts use earlier games only and never season stats as inputs. GLORY uses per-year gold/objective regression with full-season Record opponent adjustment; Record remains its input although Record, Luck, GLORB, unadjusted GLORY, Form and GLORY+ are sunset pages or redirects. [Season-stat evidence](../season_stats_report.md), [forecast evidence](../metric_backtest.md), [metric definitions](product.md).
- **Forecasts:** player-based game-length Elo covers every league; team strength is its main five starters plus league offset, with log-game rookie discounts, inactivity-growing Glicko-1-style RD and a 25-point major-to-minor move-down bonus. Non-major domestic updates use slope .0065; majors/international retain ln(10)/400, with K 20 throughout; Form supplies earlier-game, recency/opponent-adjusted, roster-continuity-scaled stats, and FORGE blends it with Elo within leagues but uses Elo across leagues. Committed Form/FORGE weights require offline `--write-weights` refreshes; see [backtest](../metric_backtest.md), [joint RD/slope tuning](../research/elo_glicko_scale.md) and [technical rules](tech.md).
- **International calibration:** a fitted forecast correction supplements Elo's player-carried regional offsets only for recognized cross-home international fixtures. Committed coefficients (fit through Oct 2) require no build-time fitting; new calls save version/cutoff, old calls stay frozen, and `REGIONAL_CALIBRATION=0` rolls back this layer. Broad historical gains do not establish major-v-major, 2026-only or Kalshi gains ([validation](../research/regional_calibration_validation.md), [native guards](../research/regional_calibration_benchmarks.md)).
- **Predictions and history:** Leaguepedia fixtures receive FORGE within leagues (Elo fallback without Form) and Elo across leagues, with game/series odds, saved/reconstructed scorecards, recent results and monthly archives; CLI odds match the page. Builds replay current-model calls using strictly earlier-day ratings at start-day/12h for backtests without overwriting original saved calls; alerts pin the backed team and first terms across provider/order changes, and atomic ETag persistence prevents stale publication. [Method](tech.md), [history and recovery](work_log.md).

- **Site and CLI:** daily static publishing includes metric registers, team/player histories and game logs, search, season/name/role filters, Predictions, Props and Live; cross-league head-to-head uses Elo. - **AURA:** published major-league player-season stat (20+ games), in win-chance points per game with role comparisons: lane-opponent gaps at 15 minutes plus a quarter of team-centred change to 25 (20 if the game ends first). It measures lane contribution, misses later team-level credit and adds no demonstrated signal beyond Player Elo on substitutions; [AURA report](../aura_report.md), change rules in [tech](tech.md).

No historical lines exist before collection, CS projects low, and actual-line validation remains open ([model report](../props_report.md), [implementation record](work_log.md)).
- **Market benchmark:** `evaluate_markets.py` compares reconstructed calls with Kalshi; major-league FORGE roughly ties 12h prices, loses at close, and the market leads elsewhere. The original snapshot reported 2,486 series, FORGE 0.5937 versus 0.5911 at 12h (Δ +0.0026, −0.014 to +0.019), other-league FORGE Δ +0.022 (+0.005 to +0.040), cross-league Elo +0.075 and our conditional weight 0.09 (−0.04 to +0.20); these are historical, not forward profitability evidence ([market report](../market_report.md)).
- **Live benchmark:** `evaluate_live.py` replays feed timelines and pre-map calls against Kalshi trades, guarding pre-map/pregame/early/mid/late/pooled log loss on data rebuilt weekly in CI (`live-bench.yml`; release pin as fallback). The historical snapshot had 1,666 feed maps/813 series and 3,346 pre-maps: pooled in-game Δ +0.017 (+0.006 to +0.027), conditional model weight +0.30 (+0.17 to +0.43); no held-price strategy beat zero, and exactly flat 21/30-second prices returned −14%/−16%. Trade prices do not establish executable book prices; pregame remains overconfident ([research index](../research/README.md), [release benchmark](../research/live_model_release.md)).

## Known limits

- Team and player pages’ fact lines and Elo history update only at builds.

- **Refresh:** Google Drive quotas can leave yesterday's data despite current-year-only daily downloads (all years Mondays); missing latest results suppress alerts. If refusals persist, investigate authenticated Drive copy/download or a mirror; “Games through” and build warnings expose staleness ([deployment](deployment.md)).
- **Benchmark guard:** CI enforces only “not significantly worse”; reviewers must confirm significant gains from reports, and comparisons need a base `--dump`. Market samples (~2,500 series/~1,500 map-1 games) detect only large regressions; forecast-weight drift only warns, so refit/commit remains manual ([rules](tech.md)).
- **Season stats:** only the four majors; expansion is a product decision. Shared five-game minimum understates uncertainty (GLORY needs about eight games and win % sixteen for 0.5 reliability); Luck retains some skill/repeatability, and Record counts all leagues while Luck counts majors only ([season report](../season_stats_report.md), [product](../../PRODUCT.md)).
- **Elo/Form:** league offsets combine sparse international play, team-only cross-league links and compensated transfers; Player Elo moves all starters together and cannot allocate individual credit. Form is league-relative, FORGE scales differ by league (majors share one), and Form hyperparameters (half-life 20, carry .5, prior 5) were selected on the reported backtest; out-of-year blend fitting is their only guard ([backtest](../metric_backtest.md)).
- **Register/history scope:** player registers include major/international players or those active in two years, with historical seasons restricted to major-league 10+ game seasons; a team page's current FORGE uses today's state while its season row uses the last-game state. Large team/player pages and game-log JSON remain a storage/deploy cost; changed-only uploads are shipped but new-game-day volume is unmeasured ([deployment](deployment.md), [UI](ui.md)).
- **Game logs:** series and best-of are inferred from consecutive opponents within 12h (2–0 Bo2 treated as Bo3, 1–1 has no series chance), and reconstructed calls use today's weights. Historical Kalshi prices need manual benchmark/export/upload; otherwise only prediction-log prices appear ([technical layout](tech.md)).
- **AURA coverage/meaning:** lane gaps share kill/assist credit and can yield positive death coefficients conditional on gold; full-game shares mostly reflect team roles, and substitution selection confounds tests. LPL snapshots are absent in 2016–17, 2022–25 and about half of 2021; refreshed 2026 raw data has 542/674 complete 15-minute snapshots (~20% missing), so coverage changes the comparison population and the built DB/site may lag ([report](../aura_report.md), [research decisions](work_log.md)).
- **Data/test gaps:** calendar-year seasons include autumn qualifiers in their actual year; 13 games with only one team row receive no Elo. `get_matches_frame(before=...)` is exercised by backtests without a dedicated test; e2e requires a built DB locally or in publish ([tech](tech.md)).
- **Predictions:** Leaguepedia can fail even with bot credentials, retaining yesterday's calls/results; uncovered teams cannot be rated, aliases need manual updates, and last-known rosters/daily data can be stale by one or two days. Reconstructed calls use earlier games but weights fit on those games; series odds use separate major/rest beta-binomial correlations, while older calls used independence. Keep new cross-region events in `INTERNATIONAL_LEAGUES` and labels in `EVENT_LEAGUES`; otherwise teams can disappear from FORGE/alerts ([tech](tech.md), [backtest](../metric_backtest.md)).
- **Alerts/prices:** GitHub scheduling can delay or skip hourly reads during publishing, quotes move before notification, and candidate forecasts refresh with price updates; fill issues are private. The 6h minimum has retrospective support but does not validate first-qualifying hourly signals; the old raw 5-point bar is superseded, no upper lead cap remains, and thresholds need forward validation. JS prices refresh hourly (without JS, daily); Kalshi price-redistribution terms remain unchecked, with `KALSHI_PRICES=0` available ([market report](../market_report.md), [blend validation](../research/market_blend_validation.md)).
- **Market benchmark:** reconstructed calls have weight-fit leakage; day-only start times can make close prices early, unmatched names mostly lack Oracle's Elixir coverage, six result disagreements were dropped without diagnosis, and cold candlestick fetching is slow/rate-limited ([market report](../market_report.md)).
- **Live collection:** wake-up depends on the prediction log and can miss fixtures or late starts; historical map-1 discovery gaps remain and future feed availability is provider-dependent. Estimated clocks miss pauses before joining, pregame ratings omit earlier maps that day, and pre-draft book history is absent before worker startup; books record top-five levels every 2s in draft/in game. Production match-day wake/collection/publish/shutdown still needs observation; stream embeds vary and Twitch needs the correct `parent` host (`localhost`, not `127.0.0.1`) ([deployment](deployment.md), [release validation](../research/live_model_release.md)).
