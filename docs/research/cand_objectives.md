## Limits

- Trained on the 2024 feed maps only (the gol.gg rows have no objective counts); the 2025 H2 test outcomes were seen in the v2 and v3 runs, though nothing here was tuned on them.
- The adoption rule covers the primary set; the secondary set is a declared reading, not a second test.
- Weights are not causal: towers come out slightly negative early because their value is already in gold.
- Feed counts as saved by `feed_timelines.py` (dragons is the length of the feed's dragon list; inhibitors count destroyed so far, including respawned ones). Retrospective only; not yet run on the live service's own 10-second frames.
