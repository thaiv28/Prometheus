# Series score at gold checkpoints

Generated by `scripts/research/series_checkpoint.py` on 2026-10-16. 14,189 clean Bo3 and Bo5 series, 38,234 maps (24,045 of them map 2 or later), 2018–2026. One side per map. The live call is `time_model_v1` with Elo from before the series, as the live page uses it; 0 minutes is its pre-map call.

Fitted on maps 2+ of 2018–2024, scored on maps 2+ of 2025–2026. LL change is log loss per map against the live call (the fitted lead against the live call refit alone; the Beta prior against the raw call); negative is better. 95% intervals resample whole series. Maps that ended before a checkpoint drop out of it.
