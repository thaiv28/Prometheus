Each arm and the overall winner are selected only on tuning results before held-out scoring.

One extension was fixed in advance: at each arm's initial winner, retain its coordinates and add the outward value for every boundary coordinate, testing their local Cartesian product once (floor 40/140, ratio 1.1/3.25, growth 0/55, information 0.125/2). Pre-update RD²/RD_min² multiplies moves: at the floor the RD weight is exactly 1. Without legacy multipliers a settled player moves at K=20 even beside or against uncertain players. With legacy multipliers enabled the existing provisional/away factors also apply. No side normalization: applied team means can differ (as with published provisional/away factors); discount decay also affects effective changes. League offsets, K and transfers stay published. No g(RD), league RD or volatility estimation.

At E=0.5 without inactivity, RD 250→100 takes about 11 games at m=1, 21 at m=0.5 and 41 at m=0.25. Thus slower information shrinkage is explicitly tested. The wider floor/ratio grid changes this duration too.

Tuning replays use the chronological prefix (49,342 of 102,929 games), including intervening differently labelled seasons. Published prefix equality is exact; selected RD prefixes are checked against full replays before held-out scoring. Checkpoints and the pre-score frozen protocol/choices live under gitignored `data/research/elo_glicko/v2/`.

Scoring follows the existing research harness: leave-one-year-out win curves within each requested partition; Form opponent adjustment and Form/FORGE weights are refit for every selected replay. For major sections Form fits use the requested partition; the shared within-other-league helper fits Form feature weights on all other major-league years in the full frame, including tuning years. This is not a fully chronological deployment simulation. Intervals are 95% paired bootstraps over individual games (not series); sections and secondary arms are not multiplicity-adjusted.

## Verdict and limits

**Iterate; do not ship. Passes the benchmark significance gate.** Overall tuning winner's significant gains: Elo, other leagues, FORGE, other leagues; significant regressions: none. Shipping requires a significant targeted gain and no significant guarded regression; secondary-arm results cannot replace the tuning-selected winner. RD models uncertainty, while the rookie discount models a shifted prior. A failed replacement need not invalidate the discount. The selected floor and information multiplier remain at expanded boundaries; iterate on that unresolved tuning choice before selecting production constants. In normalized variance units the effective parameters are new/floor, c/floor and m × floor², so floor and information trade off rather than being independent mechanisms. No optional league-RD experiment or full Glicko-2 volatility fit was attempted.

V1 also normalized side means, which could change a settled player's effective K when lineup uncertainty differed. V2 removes that normalization to enforce the requested per-player weight literally. An initial v2 tuning attempt retaining normalization was aborted before held-out scoring; its scores were discarded and the same grid restarted with direct weights.
