# Elo fitted scale (2026-10-19)

Generated by `scripts/research/elo_fitted_scale.py`. Research only; published Elo, DB and weight artifacts unchanged.

Tune 2014–2021; report 2022–2026. Fixed grid: slope {ln(10)/400, .0070, .0085, .0100} × K {12,16,20,24,28}; tier {.0085 majors/international, .0103 other domestic} × the same K grid. Third design: non-major domestic slope {ln(10)/400,.0070,.0085,.0103} × K {20,24,28,32}; major/international slope ln(10)/400 and K 20. One training-only edge extension: slope .0045/.0121, K 16/36 on the selected lower/upper edge, scoring all new Cartesian cells once. league_factors = K_other/20 scales only players; international callbacks and offset rules stay native (offset values can differ through prior ratings). The published slope is exactly ln(10)/400, not the rounded .00576. Tuning minimizes games-weighted domestic + within-other-league Elo log loss.

All forecast curves are refit per replay: out-of-year Elo, shrunk per-league slopes, variant-adjusted Form and FORGE blends. As in the prior harness, held-out curves use other 2022+ seasons; these are rating-design comparisons, not an earlier-only deployment simulation. Other-league FORGE's Form weights follow the existing helper (all other years, including tuning years). International regional calibration is disabled only inside this experiment: its frozen artifact replaces the refitted probability with an old-scale slope. Both Elo and FORGE therefore use refitted curves, as the original research protocol did. The baseline means published Elo ratings with refitted forecast curves, not today's calibrated international calls.

## Interpretation and limits

Significance gate (significant Elo gains within other leagues AND cross-league, no significant regression in any reported section): Non-major only. No shipping in this research change. Intervals spanning zero are inside the noise; individual game resampling does not account for series clusters or repeated historical research.

Recommendation: **iterate** with earlier-only calibration and native release guards before considering a production change.

The update targets duration-weighted actual scores, not binary wins: a 30-minute winner scores .825. Its expected-score slope need not equal the forecast slope for binary wins, even at equilibrium. Increasing the update slope compresses ratings and can steepen the refitted forecast slope; matching the old forecast coefficient is not self-consistency. Rookie discounts and league-move bonuses also remain in fixed rating points, so changing slope/K is not a pure change of units.

Replay wall time: median 7.06s, range 3.63–10.18s (41 grid replays, 102,929 games each). Reproduction 3.63s. Whole experiment 400.6s. Single process, VECLIB_MAXIMUM_THREADS=1; shared-machine load affects timing. No CI/publish/hourly work added.
