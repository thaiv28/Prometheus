### Reading

- The residual check's up-move effect doesn't survive as a fix: every up-move penalty made the tuning objective worse (a stored penalty takes points out of the major's pool). Only the down-move bonus helps, and the smallest step (25) won, so the best value may be smaller; the grid was fixed in advance and not extended.
- The fading versions are near zero everywhere and slightly worse on major v other games (30 games: +0.0027, +0.0006 to +0.0051).
- The stored bonus passes the forecast rule on the held-out seasons (significant on the targeted cross-league and within-other-league games, nothing significantly worse) and improves our market calls, but fails the PR check's domestic Elo guard over every season. Shipping it needs `benchmark-override` with that reason. Not shipped here: the options stay off by default.
