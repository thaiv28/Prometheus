# v1 (flawed: reference above floor)

Archived 2026-10-19 run: RD_ref exceeded the floor, reducing veteran K below 20. The v2 report supersedes its verdict.

Generated by `scripts/research/elo_glicko.py`. Research only; no published behavior, weights or DB changed. Negative Δ means improvement over current published Elo.

Tune 2014–2021; report 2022–2026. Fixed grid: RD_new ∈ {250,350}, RD_min=100, RD_ref ∈ {150,200}, c ∈ {5,15}: 8 candidates per arm, 24 total. Objective is games-weighted domestic plus within-other-league Elo log loss. Each arm and the overall winner were selected using only tuning results before any held-out scoring.

RD² grows by c² × days inactive (capped at RD_new²), then after each game shrinks to max(100², 1/(1/RD² + q² E(1−E))), q=ln(10)/400. The pre-update RD²/RD_ref² multiplies the player's move. Per-side weights (including enabled provisional/away multipliers) are normalized to the shared mean of the two side means: equal/opposite mean stored-rating changes. Rookie discount decay can still make effective changes non-zero-sum. League offset changes, K and transfer adjustments stay published. No g(RD), league RD or volatility estimation; this is not full Glicko-2.

Scoring follows the existing research harness: leave-one-year-out win curves within each requested partition; Form opponent adjustment and Form/FORGE weights are refit for every selected replay. This is not a fully chronological deployment simulation. Intervals are 95% paired bootstraps over individual games (not series); sections and secondary arms are not multiplicity-adjusted.

## Verdict and limits

**Drop this fixed design; do not ship.** Overall tuning winner's significant gains: none; significant regressions: Elo, other leagues, Elo, cross-league. Shipping requires a significant targeted gain and no significant guarded regression; secondary-arm results cannot replace the tuning-selected winner. RD models uncertainty, while the rookie discount models a shifted prior. A failed replacement need not invalidate the discount. No optional league-RD experiment or full Glicko-2 volatility fit was attempted.
