## Limits

- Matchup and synergy (in the primary shift) were kept after draft_score.py's pre-match 2025–26 test, which overlaps this test; the round-one shift, chosen before that, does not meet the rule on its own.
- Maps dropped for missing champions are mostly LPL (Oracle's Elixir lacks champion rows for many LPL games: 418 of 959 from July 2025 in this DB), so the LPL is under-represented in every partition.
- The live service does not compute the draft: Riot's window has each player's `championId` in `gameMetadata` (kept by `feed.py` as `participant_metadata`) from about 30 s into the game; a live input needs champion ids mapped to Oracle's Elixir names, feed players mapped to OE player ids, and each player's and champion's history read from the DB before the map.
- The saved feed timelines have no champions, so this joins the shift by OE game id; picks as the feed reports them are untested.
