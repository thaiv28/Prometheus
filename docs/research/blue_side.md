## Blue side in Elo and our calls (2026-10-12)

Generated by `scripts/research/blue_side.py`.

**Verdict: nothing ships.** A side term in Elo's update (one advantage, one per tier, or learned online) changes held-out log loss by at most 0.0005 in any section, every interval but one spanning 0; the learned version is slightly worse on cross-league games (+0.0004, +0.0001 to +0.0008). Blue's edge is about 15–17 Elo points in the update, and teams play both sides about equally over a season, so ratings already come out nearly unbiased. For our calls, game 1's side can't be read off Leaguepedia's `team1` (blue in 56% of 394 logged matches; only LAS and VCS list the blue team first), so a map-1 side term has no input. In later games the previous game's loser picks, and that choice swings by season (loser on blue in 92% of major-league games in 2025, 21% in 2026), but it moves a series call by under 0.1 point. A per-game side term would only help live calls for later maps, which we don't make.
