Generated by `scripts/evaluate_markets.py` on 2026-10-04. Market: Kalshi (`KXLOLGAME` series winners, `KXLOLMAP` single maps). Matches from 11 Dec 2025 to 30 Sep 2026.

Our call is reconstructed from games before the match day, as the Predictions page does for matches it missed; the forecast weights were fit on data that includes these matches, which flatters us a little. The market's chance is the mid of the bid and ask, averaged over the two teams' contracts, left out when no quote has a spread of 0.10 or less. Δ is ours minus the market's: a negative Δ log loss or Δ Brier means we did better. Intervals are paired bootstraps over matches.
